INFORMED VOLUNTARY CONSENT 
to Receive Remote Medical Services to the Public Offer Agreement
This Consent has been drawn up in accordance with Article 77 of Section 2 and Article 134 of Section 3 of the Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Public Health and the Healthcare System" dated July 7, 2020, No. 360-VI LRK.
I hereby consent to the provision of online (remote) medical services (consultations) to me via telecommunications means.
This document constitutes the Patient's informed consent to receive remote medical services from the Group of Companies and defines the terms and conditions under which the Patient consents to consultations with recommendations, as well as other medical services using remote technologies on the internet resource (hereinafter referred to as the Web Portal). I agree that remote medical services include specialist consultations, test interpretation, issuance of recommendations, and obtaining a second opinion from a specialist.
By accepting the terms of the Public Offer Agreement (by performing acceptance on the Web Portal), I am informed and confirm that I agree to the following:
1. When online (remote) medical services (consultations) are provided to me in the absence of a previously established diagnosis:
1.1. A medical worker of the Group of Companies may provide me only with general reference information on medical issues that are the subject of my request, and this information cannot replace an in-person appointment with my attending physician upon visiting a medical organization, which may be required. Consequently, under no circumstances can the information I receive be used by me for self-treatment and self-diagnosis.
1.2. No diagnosis of an existing disease will be established, and no treatment will be prescribed to me during the provision of online (remote) medical services (consultations). In such cases, the purpose of the service shall be to collect and analyze complaints and anamnesis, provide me with recommendations on the need for certain examinations, and make a decision on the necessity of an in-person appointment;
2. When online (remote) medical services (consultations) are provided to me in the presence of a previously established diagnosis:
2.1. Recommendations (prescriptions) will be based on the assumption that the previously established medical diagnosis of the disease is correct or largely correct, provided that it is also confirmed by the data of conducted examinations (anamnesis, test results, medical reports) that I provide and upon which the specialist's medical report will be based;
2.2. The physician has the right to question the previously established medical diagnosis and prescribe repeated or additional examinations;
2.3. Based on the results of repeated or additional examinations, the specialist may adjust or completely change the treatment prescribed to me, as well as refuse to issue a report if the specialist is not confident in the correctness of the diagnosis or if it is impossible to establish a correct diagnosis for me. As a result, I may be given recommendations to conduct an in-person appointment with a specialist, including for the purpose of deciding on surgical intervention.
3. I agree:
	•	To provide the medical worker with complete, reliable, and exhaustive information about my state of health (complaints, past illnesses, allergic reactions, medications taken, etc.);
	•	To ensure on my part a high-quality technical connection (stable internet, working camera/microphone) for conducting the remote consultation;
	•	To strictly follow the physician's recommendations and timely inform them of any changes in my state of health

4. I agree that:
	•	Concealing or distorting health status information may lead to an incorrect assessment of symptoms and the ineffectiveness (or harm) of prescribed recommendations;
	•	The physician has the right to terminate the remote consultation and recommend an immediate in-person appointment or calling an ambulance (103) if my current state of health requires an in-person examination
5. I am informed of the potential risks of using telemedicine technologies, including (but not limited to):
	•	The limitations of online examination (impossibility of palpation, auscultation, direct physical examination);
	•	The risk of potential technical failures, communication interruptions, or degradation of audio/video signal transmission quality related to internet quality;
	•	The necessity of a repeat or in-person appointment to clarify the diagnosis.
6. The Group of Companies guarantees the confidentiality of the transmitted information and the security of personal data in accordance with the requirements of the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
7. I am notified that for quality control purposes and to protect the rights of the Parties, text chats, audio, and video recordings of consultations shall be recorded and stored on secure servers of the Group of Companies in compliance with the medical confidentiality regime, in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
8. I confirm that in the event of a sudden sharp deterioration in my health or the occurrence of life-threatening symptoms (severe retrosternal pain, speech disorders, loss of consciousness, heavy bleeding, shortness of breath, etc.), I will immediately call an ambulance team at number 103 or contact the nearest hospital emergency department.
9. This informed consent shall enter into force from the moment I confirm it on the Web Portal — after registration and checking the box in the corresponding field regarding consent to the public offer agreement, informed consent, and consent to the collection and processing of personal data, which constitutes its acceptance. I agree that this informed consent remains valid for the entire time I use the Web Portal and receive medical services.
