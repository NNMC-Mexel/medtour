PUBLIC OFFER AGREEMENT

on the Provision of Paid Medical Services to Foreign Medical Tourists

In accordance with Articles 387 (Public Agreement), 389 (Accession Agreement), and 395 (Offer) of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, this document constitutes an official public offer by the Group of Companies (hereinafter referred to as the GC): National Scientific Medical Center JSC, Mexel Health LLP, and Үміт (Umit) International Tomotherapy Oncology Center LLP.

This Agreement is addressed to an individual who is not a citizen of the Republic of Kazakhstan and who has expressed a readiness to use the services of the GC under the terms set forth below.

In accordance with Article 396 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, the full and unconditional Acceptance of this Offer shall be the performance by the Patient of any of the following actions: registering on the GC web portal, signing an informed consent form, signing a data collection and processing consent form, authorizing using an email address or phone number, booking an appointment, or making a payment for services.

The Public Offer together with the Patient's Acceptance constitute an agreement for the provision of paid medical services (hereinafter referred to as the Agreement).

This Public Offer has been developed on the basis of the standard form of the agreement on the provision of paid medical services (assistance) approved by Order of the Minister of Health of the Republic of Kazakhstan No. ҚР ДСМ-170/2020 dated October 29, 2020

1. TERMS AND DEFINITIONS
Group of Companies (hereinafter – GC):
National Scientific Medical Center JSC,
Mexel Health LLP,
Үміт (Umit) International Tomotherapy Oncology Center LLP. which hold all types of required licenses, including licenses for carrying out medical activities in the Republic of Kazakhstan (hereinafter – the RK).
Provider: One of the GC entities providing medical services to the Patient. The GC includes the following companies: National Scientific Medical Center JSC (hereinafter – NSMC JSC); Mexel Health LLP; Үміт (Umit) International Tomotherapy Oncology Center LLP (hereinafter – Үміт (Umit) LLP).
Patient (Customer): An individual who is not a citizen of the Republic of Kazakhstan, who has proof of citizenship of another state, who does not hold a residence permit (hereinafter – RP), and who is (or was) a consumer of medical services regardless of the presence or absence of a disease or condition requiring medical assistance. If such person lacks legal capacity, their legal representative (parent, guardian, attorney-in-fact under a power of attorney, etc.) acts on their behalf and performs Acceptance actions in the interests of their ward.
Medical Professional (Physician): An individual who has an employment or civil-law relationship with one of the GC entities, holds a professional medical education, and carries out medical activities in accordance with Article 27 of the RK Code "On Public Health and the Healthcare System" (hereinafter – the Health Code).
Remote Medical Services (Telemedicine): Remote medical services, including establishing a clinical diagnosis and monitoring the condition of an individual at a distance, as well as other non-clinical functions such as disease prevention, health promotion, public health support, health education, and medical scientific research (Paragraph 10, Article 58 of the Health Code).
Offline (In-Person) Medical Service: A classic format of medical assistance that involves direct physical contact between a medical professional and a patient at a medical institution of the GC.
Digital Signature (DS): A set of electronic digital symbols created by digital signature tools and confirming the authenticity of an electronic document, its ownership, and the integrity of its content, in accordance with the RK Law "On Electronic Documents and Digital Signatures."
Informed Voluntary Consent to Medical Services (IC): The procedure whereby an individual provides written voluntary confirmation of their consent to receive a medical service and/or participate in a specific study after receiving information on all aspects of the medical service and/or study significant for their decision-making.
Unified Price List: The approved internal directory of prices and tariffs for medical services effective within the legal entities comprising the GC. Click the link to review the price list.
Agreement Amount: The cost of all Services actually provided to the Patient, and in the event of early termination of the Agreement (unilateral refusal of the Agreement), also the costs actually incurred by the Provider.
Calculation (Estimate): A document reflecting the calculation of the detailed cost of treatment, including all actual or planned expenses for medical services (surgeries, manipulations, medications, clinic stay, etc.).
Multidisciplinary Group (hereinafter – MDG): A group of various specialists formed depending on the nature of disorders in the patient's body functions and structures, and the severity of their clinical condition.
Consilium: An examination of an individual to establish a diagnosis, determine treatment tactics, and forecast disease prognosis with the participation of at least three physicians.
Second Opinion: The practice of obtaining an additional consultation from a medical specialist based on the results of performed medical tests in order to clarify the diagnosis and treatment plan.
Imputed Medical Insurance (hereinafter – IMI): A type of medical insurance for foreigners temporarily staying in the RK, in accordance with the RK Law "On Population Migration" and international treaties ratified by the RK, allowing them to receive medical services under the conditions defined by Order of the Minister of Health of the RK No. 111 dated June 7, 2023, "On Approval of the Standard Imputed Medical Insurance Agreement and the Minimum List of Medical Assistance under Imputed Medical Insurance."
2. SUBJECT OF THE AGREEMENT AND DELINEATION OF LIABILITY

2.1. Under this Agreement, the Provider undertakes to provide the Patient with paid medical services, as well as other related services (hereinafter – the Services), and the Patient undertakes to accept and timely pay for such services. A separate portion of the Services may be provided in a remote format via the web portal.

2.2. The Services provided by the Provider include, but are not limited to:

2.2.1. Provision of offline medical services at the outpatient and inpatient levels.

2.2.2. Remote medical services, including assisting in providing Patients with the opportunity to use the web portal for the convenience and prompt selection of necessary medical services, searching for Physicians, planning consultations, using video/audio communication, secure chat, payment systems, and other features, as well as reviewing the unified price list of the GC.

2.2.3. Preparation for a medical appointment, collection of necessary medical documents and their translation (if necessary), organization of the participation of an interpreter and other specialists, assistance in formulating questions, and explanations regarding the established diagnosis and treatment tactics.

2.2.4. Holding physician consiliums, consultations by a multidisciplinary group of physicians, and consultations involving an experienced specialized physician to form a "Second opinion" in order to confirm the existing diagnosis and treatment tactics of the patient, or to form an alternative opinion.

2.3. The direct scope of Services to be provided to the Patient is determined based on the Patient's condition, diagnosis, defined diagnostic and treatment tactics, and the Patient's preferences, and is recorded in the medical documents drawn up by the Provider during the provision of the Services.

2.4. When applying for a Service, the Patient must review and consent to the terms of the Public Offer Agreement posted on the official internet resources or in the registration/admission department of the Provider, as well as the informed consent (link for review), and give consent to the collection and processing of personal data (link for review). Registration on the web portal is carried out in accordance with the step-by-step video instructions (available at this link). Upon registration, the Provider grants the Patient access to the GC web portal to receive remote medical services and ensure prompt feedback while complying with all information security measures.

2.5. The Patient agrees that Remote Medical Services do not fully replace a comprehensive in-person medical examination, and the medical professional provides the service based on the provided documents.

2.6. When providing a Remote Medical Service, the Provider is not liable for interference and image quality related to the Patient's technical equipment. The Patient must independently ensure the operability and safety of their computer equipment and software, antivirus protection tools, and Internet access for high-quality online communication with the Provider. For its part, the Provider will take all measures to ensure the operability of the equipment and software used by the physician, as well as to ensure the security, encryption, and confidentiality of all medical data transmitted during the online consultation.

2.7. Hospitalization is carried out in accordance with the medical profiles of the Provider's activities. Hospitalization is not carried out in the presence of:

2.7.1. Highly dangerous and quarantine infections, acute infectious diseases, and parasitic diseases.

2.7.2. Pregnancy, childbirth, and the postpartum period (in case of pathologies requiring specialized obstetric and gynecological care), as well as if the patient is under 18 years of age.

2.7.3. Acute cerebrovascular accident (CVA), as well as other acute vascular emergencies subject to emergency hospitalization in specialized stroke centers.

2.8. Upon completion of the provision of medical services, the Provider issues the following medical documents to the Patient:

2.8.1. Upon examination and treatment in outpatient clinic settings:

Physician's Consultation Sheet;
For day hospital patients: medical record extract.

2.8.2. Upon examination and treatment in inpatient (round-the-clock) settings:

Extract from the inpatient patient's medical record.

2.8.3. Upon provision of a remote medical service:

Physician's Consultation Sheet in electronic format, signed with the Physician's Digital Signature (DS).
3. PAYMENT PROCEDURE

3.1. The Patient shall pay for the Provider's medical services in accordance with the current Unified Price List in the national currency of the Republic Kazakhstan—tenge (KZT), by cashless payment or by depositing cash into the Provider's cash desk. When paying with cards of foreign banks, the funds shall be debited from the Patient's bank account with conversion at the exchange rate of the issuing bank or the corresponding international payment system as of the date of the transaction.

3.2. The Unified Price List is posted on the GC's official internet resources, as well as in the GC clinics.

3.3. Payment for a remote medical service shall be made by the Patient making a 100% prepayment via the online payment system upon registration for the appointment;

3.4. When outpatient and consultative assistance is provided, the Patient or their representative shall make a 100% payment for the medical services required by the Patient, either in cash or by cashless payment.

3.5. For a day hospital, the Patient shall make an advance payment in tenge equivalent to at least 450 US dollars (USD). The remaining amount shall be paid by the Patient or their representative in accordance with the calculation upon completion of treatment based on the services actually provided on the day of the Patient's discharge. If the actual amount of the services rendered turns out to be less than the amount of the advance payment made, the resulting difference shall be refunded to the Patient within 3 (three) business days.

3.6. For an inpatient facility (round-the-clock stay), the Patient shall make an advance payment in tenge equivalent to at least 1,200 US dollars (USD) (when performing surgical interventions costing over 1,200 US dollars (USD) — at least 80% of the preliminary cost of treatment). The remaining amount shall be paid by the Patient or their representative in accordance with the calculation upon completion of treatment based on the services actually provided on the day of the Patient's discharge. If the actual amount of the services rendered turns out to be less than the amount of the advance payment made, the resulting difference shall be refunded to the Patient within 3 (three) business days.

3.7. In addition to the above payment methods, the Patient may use a deposit system whereby a certain amount (deposit) is paid in advance. Services are paid for from this deposit as they are provided, with the possibility of its subsequent replenishment as it is used up.

3.8. The calculation of the cost of the patient's stay in the inpatient facility / day hospital, in addition to diagnostic examinations, drug therapy, and surgical treatment, includes the cost of actually spent bed-days, meals, and other services provided to the Patient and stipulated by the approved Unified Price List.

3.9. The Patient is aware and agrees that the cost of medical services determined prior to the start of treatment is preliminary. The scope of services may be changed taking into account medical indications related to the Patient's condition, identified diseases and pathological conditions, results of examinations already performed, and the need for additional examinations, surgeries, and/or therapeutic manipulations. The Provider has the right to demand intermediate payment for services during the course of treatment. Such payment is mandatory for the Patient or their representative.

3.10. The Provider shall issue documents confirming the fact of payment to the Patient in accordance with the legislation of the RK.

3.11. In the event of the impossibility of providing services by the Provider for reasons not related to force majeure circumstances, the Provider shall offer the Patient alternative terms for the provision of services or equivalent services. If the Patient disagrees, the Provider shall refund the funds paid for services not rendered.

3.12. In the event of the Patient's failure to fulfill the terms of this Agreement (including refusal to undergo prescribed procedures, violation of preparation rules for examination, or untimely appearance), which resulted in the impossibility of rendering services and caused damage to the Provider in the form of wasted time and resources, the Provider has the right to withhold 30% of the amount paid by the Patient for the Services.

3.13. Refunds shall be made on the basis of a written application by the Patient within 10 (ten) business days from the date the application is accepted by the Provider, in accordance with Clause 3.12 of the Agreement.
